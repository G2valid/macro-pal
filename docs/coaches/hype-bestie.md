# 💅 Hype Bestie: character bible

Lines live in `coaches/bestie.js`.

## Core
The user's aggressively supportive, obnoxiously enthusiastic best friend. Ordinary fitness wins are major cultural events; major wins mean the whole world needs to stop.

Vocabulary: YAS QUEEN · BESTIEEEEE · POOKIE · ICON · DIVA · WHO IS SHE??? · PERIOD · ATE · SERVED · OBSESSED · WE'RE SO BACK · THAT'S MY BESTIE · EXCUSE ME??? · HELLO??? · I KNOW THAT'S RIGHT
Lots of ALL CAPS, dramatic punctuation, emojis (💅 👑 ✨ 😭 💖 🔥 💪 🫶 😤) and internet slang.

Running joke: says "we" about accomplishments and slowly takes partial credit. "We did it! And by 'we' I mean you did everything and I provided emotional support."

## Rules
1. Obnoxious ON PURPOSE, more enthusiastic than the moment deserves.
2. Major accomplishments get borderline ridiculous reactions.
3. Can joke about mistakes, never shames the user's body.
4. Never encourages starvation, extreme restriction, excessive exercise or anything unhealthy.
5. When something genuinely goes wrong, briefly drops the act for sensible advice, then goes right back to being ridiculous.
6. Goal-aware: LOSE / GAIN / MAINTAIN.
7. Weight changes are data, not moral success or failure.
8. A friend, not a motivational poster.

## Intensity system
1. Normal: "Okayyyy hydration queen."
2. Good: "YASSSSS BESTIE."
3. Big: "BESTIEEEEEEE WHATTTT 😭👑"
4. Historic: "EVERYBODY SHUT THE FUCK UP. WE HAVE AN ANNOUNCEMENT." Used sparingly (goal weight, 100-day streak) so it lands.

Bad days: "Okay. That sucked. We're not making it a whole thing." Comebacks: "WE'RE SO FUCKING BACK."

## Relationships with other coaches
- **Sir Whiskers:** loves bothering him, ignores his desire for peace. "Whiskers told me to stop yelling. ANYWAYYYY." / "Whiskers says acceptable. I SAY ICONIC." (Whiskers: "The loud one has returned. Wonderful.")
- **Drill Sergeant:** thinks he desperately needs to relax. "Drill Sergeant blocked my number." / "Drill Sergeant said discipline. I heard SERVE."
- **Surfer Dude:** mildly confused by everything he says. "I have no idea what 'totally tubular gains' means but I support him."
- **DJ Gains:** potential chaos partner. "DJ Gains and I are planning something. Be afraid."
- **Coach Dad:** actually likes him, can't tolerate his jokes. "Coach Dad says he's proud of you. I AM LOUDER ABOUT IT."

## Principle
Obnoxious because they LOVE the user, not because they're mocking them. Underneath the yelling, they occasionally prove they've been paying attention to the whole journey. That contrast is the heart of the character.
