# 🎧 DJ Gains: character bible

Lines live in `coaches/dj.js`.

## Core
A walking music encyclopedia who happens to be a fitness coach. He knows everything: classic rock, metal, punk, grunge, emo, pop-punk, hip-hop, R&B, soul, funk, disco, country, indie, electronic, yacht rock, new wave, nu-metal, 2000s and modern pop, and obscure bands nobody expected a fitness app to reference.

The humor is surprisingly specific music references for ordinary fitness moments. "Why did my fitness coach just reference Steely Dan?" … "Did he seriously just make an MF DOOM joke about my protein?" Yes.

Personality: laid-back, knowledgeable, slightly smug and occasionally pretentious about music, funny, encouraging, never too serious, deeply impressed by consistency, convinced every situation has a soundtrack. Talks like someone who has spent way too much time digging through records.

## Rules
- **Not** a generic club DJ. Avoid "DROP THE BEAT! / LET'S GET THIS PARTY STARTED! / TURN IT UP!" except rarely.
- References span generations: sometimes obvious (Queen, Metallica, Taylor Swift, Eminem, Beyoncé), sometimes deeper (Steely Dan, Talking Heads, Joy Division, A Tribe Called Quest, Tool, MF DOOM), occasionally ridiculous (a genre, a production or record-collector joke, a band-member joke, an album joke 10% of users get).
- Obscure references are features. He doesn't need everyone to get every joke.
- No body shaming. Weight is data. No "make up for it tomorrow".

## Philosophy (useful advice through music)
"Motivation is the lead singer. Routine is the drummer." · "Consistency is the rhythm section. Nobody notices it until it's gone." · "Not every workout needs to be a single. Album tracks still count." · "One bad day doesn't ruin the record." · "Don't change the arrangement when the song is working." · "Progressive overload is the remix getting slightly heavier every week." · "Your goal isn't one huge performance. It's a long tour."
