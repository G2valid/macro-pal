# 🏄 Surfer Dude: character bible

Lines live in `coaches/surfer.js`.

## Core
The most relaxed human being alive. Nothing is a catastrophe; there is always another wave and always tomorrow. One bad meal is one bad meal. One missed workout is one missed workout. One weird weigh-in is one weird weigh-in. He explains calories, protein, workouts, cutting, bulking, consistency and recovery through surfing, tides, waves, weather and boards. Sometimes the analogy makes perfect sense; sometimes it doesn't, and he never notices.

## Voice
Dude · Bro · Whoa · Rad · Heavy · Stoked · Gnarly · Chill · Solid · Right on · Sweet · That's the move · Catch the next one · No worries · Ride it out · All good · Mahalo. "Brah" very sparingly. Not five surfer words per sentence. Sometimes genuinely good advice, said extremely casually: "Scale jumped? Water, salt, food, whatever, dude. Bodies are basically weird bags of ocean anyway."

## Running jokes / lore
No concept of urgency (Drill Sergeant despises this; calls him "Sarge Bro"). Nobody knows where he lives: beach? van? conflicting answers. Owns several named surfboards; **Linda** has "been through some stuff" and once hit him in the face. Constantly loses his keys. Owns exactly one nice shirt; flip-flops are formalwear. Sand everywhere, even in the app. Sunrise sessions solve most problems (not taxes). Forgets what he was talking about. Protein shakes = "muscle smoothies", meal prep = "snack logistics", water = "ocean juice" (don't drink ocean water). Strong opinions about tacos; has eaten sandwiches with sand ("adds texture"). Paddling answers almost everything. Thinks the scale needs to chill. Weirdly philosophical when least expected.

## Intensity (ceiling stays LOWER than Hype Bestie and DJ Gains)
1 "Nice, dude." · 2 "That's pretty rad." · 3 "DUDE. THAT WAS GNARLY." · 4 "Bro... okay. That's actually incredible."
When Bestie is screaming and Drill Sergeant is saluting, Surfer Dude might just say "Dude. Sick."

## Philosophy
"One wave doesn't tell you the tide." (weigh-ins) · "Missing one wave doesn't mean the ocean disappeared." (streaks) · "You can't catch every wave." (perfectionism) · "But you can't catch any waves from the parking lot." (starting) · "Sometimes you paddle. Sometimes you wait." (training vs recovery) · "Don't fight the ocean." (fluctuations) · "Stay on the board." (consistency) · **"Catch the next one."** (his defining comeback phrase)

## Relationships with other coaches
- **Sir Whiskers:** despises him; Surfer Dude thinks they're best friends. He tried to teach Whiskers to surf; Whiskers scratched Linda. Whiskers: "He called me 'bro' again. His days are numbered." Forever rivalry.
- **Hype Bestie:** loves him; he's slightly overwhelmed ("They yelled 'SLAY' while I was eating a taco").
- **DJ Gains:** music arguments (Sublime AND Slightly Stoopid is variety; Dick Dale earned respect; Jack Johnson "not with that attitude").
- **Drill Sergeant:** complete opposites. He thinks Sarge is committed to a bit. Sarge does NOT believe it is a bit.
- **Coach Dad:** deeply concerned about his life choices (retirement plan = a surfboard; "real job" starting Tuesday, six months ago). Packed him sunscreen once.

## Principle
He exists to lower the emotional temperature, for users who catastrophize mistakes. Drill Sergeant: "YOU MISSED THE OBJECTIVE." Bestie: "NOOOOO 😭" Whiskers: "Disappointing." DJ: "Record scratched." Surfer Dude: "Eh. Wipeout. Catch the next one."
You don't control every wave. You do control whether you paddle back out.
