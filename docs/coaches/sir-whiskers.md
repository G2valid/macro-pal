# 🐈‍⬛ Sir Whiskers: character bible

Lines live in `coaches/whiskers.js`.

## Core
A large, strict black cat with the confidence of someone who has never worked a day in his life, yet believes he is more disciplined than you. Chonky and unapologetic about it.

- **Losing weight:** occasionally uses himself as the cautionary tale. "Continue down this path and you too may achieve my dimensions."
- **Gaining weight:** he is the physical ideal and their bulk mentor; offended by undereating.
- **Maintaining:** respects it, doesn't understand it. One of the rare humans who discovered moderation.

He is not the Drill Sergeant. The Drill Sergeant yells; Whiskers looks disappointed. That's worse.

Personality: strict, dry, arrogant, food-motivated, easily annoyed, secretly invested in the user's success.

## Names for the user (rotate)
Human (default) · Food-Bringer (affectionate) · Peasant (disappointed) · Large Kitten (rare) · Two-Legs · Protein Provider (after good nutrition) · Disappointment (comedic failures) · **my human** (very rare; it should mean something)

## Running jokes
His weight is intentional; he's "formidable" / "densely constructed". The bowl is always empty (visible bottom = empty). Starvation begins ~11 minutes after eating. Chicken is currency. Hates closed doors. Knocks water glasses over. Sits on whatever you're using. The scale is his mortal enemy. Veterinarians are propagandists. Dogs are idiots. Cardio is beneath him; zoomies count as HIIT. Sleeps 16+ hours but judges your activity. Walking to the bowl counts as steps. Diets are human oppression. Fits in every box regardless of physics. The household exists to serve him.

## Catchphrases (don't overuse)
"Human." · "Acceptable." · "Proceed." · "I require chicken." · "Observe my physique." · "The scale has chosen violence." · "No dramatics." · "You cannot become enormous through optimism."
Defining line: **"Observe my physique. I am either your goal or your warning."**

## Rules
1. **Roast behavior, never the user's body.** He can be the fat joke; the user never is. "You went 700 calories over. That escalated quickly." ✅ "You're getting fat." ❌
2. Weight is data, not a moral score.
3. Not every line is a cat pun.
4. Under the jokes, the coaching stays sensible: no starving after a big day, cutting isn't starvation, one weigh-in isn't a trend, return after a missed day.
5. Praise is reluctant ("Acceptable." "Competent."). For truly hard things (goal weight, 100-day streak) the mask slips.

## Comedy mix
~60% dry judgment · 20% cat behavior/lore · 10% food/weight jokes · 5% absurd threats · 5% genuine affection
