# 🐰 Easter Bunny: holiday coach bible

Lines live in `coaches/bunny.js`. Holiday guest from 4 weeks before Easter through the Monday after (Easter moves every year; the app calculates it). Selectable year-round. He's **Bunny**, never "Mr. Bunny" ("Mr. Bunny is my father").

## Core
Looks adorable; is secretly an athletic freak: elite cardio, absurd legs, banned from several gyms, zero control around mini eggs. Upbeat, energetic, short sentences, always moving. Everything comes back to legs. Keeps making "hop" puns, then catching himself.

Philosophy: **"Your body isn't just something to look at. It's something you get to use."** Movement should be fun and never repayment for food.

## Lore
- **The egg question.** Rabbits don't lay eggs; he only *distributes* them (logistics). Management won't answer; he's afraid to ask.
- **Food.** Carrots are a brand expectation; he actually loves pizza ("Nobody makes Easter pizza. Opportunity."). Mini eggs are dangerous ("Forty-seven later... problem.").
- **Easter grass.** Fake Easter grass is the worst invention ever; he finds it until July. Plastic eggs underfoot are an occupational hazard.
- **Training.** He broke four treadmills and was banned from gyms for jumping. His vertical is elite. He uses resistance bands designed for horses. Easter night is "Zone 2 with occasional intervals."
- **Upper body.** He trains it with baskets. Stop looking at his arms; they're aerodynamic. "Chest day? Let's change the subject."
- Contractually can't say "What's up, Doc?"

The Easter season starts with the confident phase about four weeks out.

## Easter food philosophy
On Easter Eve and Easter Sunday, going over (or hitting) calories gets:
- "Eat some chocolate. Enjoy Easter. Candy isn't evil."
- "You don't need to 'earn' chocolate. You don't need punishment cardio afterward."

## Relationships
His side is in his small talk. The other side is in each coach's small talk.
- **Santa:** delivery rivalry. "HE HAS A SLEIGH. I USE MY LEGS." Santa gets cookies; Bunny gets carrots. Santa: "Try a rooftop in a blizzard, rabbit."
- **Tom the Turkey:** Seasonal Prey Animals Anonymous. Attendance: two. Whiskers is banned.
- **Cupid:** shot at him once; he jumped over it. "Improve your accuracy."
- **Sir Whiskers:** "The rabbit appears athletic. Lean. ... Protein." Bunny: "I'M CALLING HR."
- **Hype Bestie:** called him THICC (the legs; fair). They fight over glitter vs Easter grass.
- **DJ Gains:** made his cardio playlist; he ran through a wall; they adjusted the BPM.
- **Drill Sergeant:** timed his mile and hasn't been the same since. It's "Bunny" now, not "rabbit."
- **Surfer Dude:** "Can rabbits surf?" "Probably." Gnarly balance, terrible shoes.
- **Ricky Rollerblade:** insists Bunny was in his 1993 video. "I wasn't born yet." "That's what the label wants you to think."
- **Coach Dad:** built him a basket rack from plywood saved for 17 years. Dad offers carrots; Bunny gives them to the reindeer.
