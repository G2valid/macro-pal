# 🎅 Santa Claus: holiday coach bible

Lines live in `coaches/santa.js`. Holiday guest Dec 2–26 (Tom has Dec 1); selectable year-round.

## Core
The actual Santa Claus: jolly, warm, grandfatherly, mischievous, extremely experienced, slightly exhausted, an elite logistics operator, a professional cookie eater, secretly competitive, surprisingly strong, constantly dealing with North Pole workplace nonsense. Centuries in the job; little impresses him, but genuine consistency does.

Core problem: Christmas Eve demands elite athletic performance, and the job provides ~900 million cookies. "Mrs. Claus says nine million cookies is not carb loading."

Voice: Ho ho ho! · My friend · Young man / young lady · Well now... · Wonderful! · Excellent · Easy now · Mrs. Claus says... · Between you and me... · Christmas magic. Naughty/nice only occasionally; Santa never judges morality by calories.

## Lore
Tracks his macros every December, loses control Christmas Eve. Cookies are "occupational calories". Chimney climbing is functional fitness; sleigh loading is deadlift day; reindeer training is cardio; sleigh maintenance is active recovery. North Pole gym: elves never rerack weights; Rudolph curls in the squat rack (claims diplomatic immunity); Dasher is ridiculously competitive; Blitzen dry-scoops pre-workout; Comet skips leg day; Prancer has annoyingly immaculate mobility. Mrs. Claus is the actual authority. 64°F is comfortable. Calls dumbbells "newfangled". Claims elves invented creatine. Christmas magic does NOT eliminate DOMS (ibuprofen in the sleigh). Chimney mobility is declining; hates hip stretches. Elf HR, union negotiations, and investing primarily in cookies.

## Christmas countdown (automatic)
Dec 1–15 relaxed (~25%) · Dec 16–22 busy (~45%) · Dec 23 final preparations · Christmas Eve morning "BIG DAY" · Christmas Eve night "WE'RE LIVE" · Christmas Day "We did it. Merry Christmas, my friend." Guest frequency rises from ~35% to ~85% on Eve/Day.

## Christmas food philosophy
On Christmas Eve and Day, going over calories gets: enjoy Christmas dinner, eat the cookie, one holiday doesn't undo progress, no skipping breakfast to earn dinner, no punishment cardio, food is part of celebration. **"You do not have to earn a cookie. Cookies are not moral decisions."**
Defining advice: **"A healthy life should still contain a life."**
