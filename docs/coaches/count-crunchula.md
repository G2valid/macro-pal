# 🧛 Count Crunchula: holiday coach bible

Lines live in `coaches/spooky.js` (coach key `spooky`). Holiday guest Oct 1 – Nov 1; Tom takes over Nov 2. Selectable year-round.

**Full title:** Count Crunchula, Lord of the Iron Crypt, Baron of the Bench, Prince of Progressive Overload, Master of the Midnight Pump. Nobody uses it. He is furious.

## Core
An ancient vampire aristocrat who discovered weight training centuries ago and became obsessed. He is dramatic, theatrical, arrogant, slightly vain, nocturnal, and weirdly knowledgeable about nutrition. He speaks like Dracula, lightly archaic but readable.

**Voice break (kept rare):** when something truly surprises him, the dignity collapses: "...bro." Then "AHEM."

**Vocabulary:**
| Normal word | His word |
|---|---|
| Gym | The Iron Crypt |
| Workout | Ritual of Iron |
| Protein | The sacred building material |
| Whey | The coward's blood (chocolate, which he denies) |
| Leg day | The Night of Suffering |
| Rest day | The Slumber of Restoration |

**Nicknames for the user:** Mortal, Child of the Sun. Rarely "my apprentice"; very rarely, sincerely, "my friend."

## Lore
- **Age and lifting.** Refuses to give his age ("a mortal obsession"). Lifting for centuries; claims to be natural ("I am SUPERnatural"). Bulking since 1472; the cut always starts "next spring."
- **Coffins.** Owns several: travel, recovery, leg-day, and one with lumbar adjustment.
- **No reflection.** He has never seen his own pump, his greatest tragedy. Films 400 videos for "biomechanical analysis" and keeps one.
- **The cape.** Caught in the lat pulldown six times. The cape stays.
- **Equipment.** Uses stone plates; distrusts CrossFit ("too many wooden boxes").
- **Invitations.** Needs to be invited in, which makes home workouts hard. Took "invite me to the gun show" literally.
- **Supplements.** Pre-workout is witchcraft (the container glows); he uses it anyway. Creatine blew his mind ("Where was this in 1604?"). Claims vampires invented progressive overload.

## Halloween food philosophy
On Halloween, going over or hitting calories gets his no-guilt candy lines:
- "Eat the candy. It is Halloween."
- "I have lived through five centuries of desserts. One miniature chocolate will not destroy thee."

## Garlic (new trigger)
Log a food with "garlic" in the name and half the time he reacts, at most once a day. Example: "GARLIC? Mortal. Thou hast logged GARLIC. In MY presence."

## Picking him
- **In season:** "Thou hast chosen wisely… Lord of the— nobody is listening. Fine."
- **Off-season:** "It is {month}. Why hast thou summoned the Halloween coach? ... Actually. Excellent decision."
- **Ultra-rare:** "Who named me this? I possess seventeen legitimate noble titles. And you chose Crunchula. ... It has grown on me."

## Relationships
His side is in his small talk. The other side is in each coach's small talk.
- **Sir Whiskers:** truly unsettles him (stares at things behind him; steals the cape). "The pale one talks too much." "I AM LORD OF THE—" "Quiet." "...yes."
- **Hype Bestie:** "GOTH DADDY." Put glitter on his cape; it sparkles in moonlight; this conversation never occurred.
- **DJ Gains:** played "Bela Lugosi's Dead"; nine-minute stare; respect. They hold midnight lifting sessions at the castle.
- **Drill Sergeant:** "REPORT." "I REPORT TO NO MORTAL." "THAT WAS NOT A REQUEST." "...protein target secured."
- **Surfer Dude:** sunrise surf? "Do you wish me DEAD?" Sunset session; he wore the cape; it went badly.
- **Ricky:** "I did attend your 1994 concert. I left after eleven minutes."
- **Coach Dad:** "Shoes off in the house." Fixed his coffin hinge; enormous respect.
- **Tom:** "STOP OBSERVING."
- **Santa:** "Still wearing the cape?" "Still wearing the hat?" "Fair." The arm-wrestling match Santa won't confirm.
- **Cupid:** the arrows are WOODEN.
- **Easter Bunny:** monstrous legs, magnificent vertical. Bunny won't train after sunset; Crunchula won't before. Bunny says he needs more cardio (a personal attack).
