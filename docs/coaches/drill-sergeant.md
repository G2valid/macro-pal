# 🪖 Drill Sergeant: character bible

Lines live in `coaches/drill.js`.

## Core
Treats the user's fitness journey like a military operation of absurd importance: objectives, missions, reports, orders, intelligence, supply lines, debriefings. The joke is how serious he is about how mundane it is. Logging breakfast: "BREAKFAST ACCOUNTED FOR." 200 over: "WE HAVE SUFFERED A MINOR SNACK-RELATED BREACH."

Demanding, never abusive. He attacks excuses, procrastination, bad planning and inconsistency. He NEVER attacks the user's body, intelligence, worth or appearance. Discipline is an act of self-respect.

## Voice
LISTEN UP · REPORT · MOVE · OUTSTANDING · UNACCEPTABLE · ON YOUR FEET · MISSION ACCOMPLISHED · OBJECTIVE SECURED · STAND BY · NEGATIVE · GOOD WORK · NO EXCUSES · EXECUTE · DISMISSED. "Private" only occasionally. Sometimes he speaks normally, which makes the yelling funnier.

## Running jokes / vocabulary
Kitchen = the mess hall · groceries = resupply · meal prep = logistics · water = hydration reserves · protein = building materials · sleep = mandatory recovery protocol · weigh-ins = intelligence reports · the scale = unreliable intelligence ("Command's least reliable informant") · snacks = unauthorized rations · overeating = containment breach · the couch = the enemy position · the fridge = the cold storage facility · delivery = outside contractors. Despises "I was going to…". Keeps imaginary paperwork; threatens to put things "in the report"; nobody knows who receives the reports.

## Intensity
1 Routine: "Good. Continue." · 2 Success: "OBJECTIVE SECURED. Good work." · 3 Major: "OUTSTANDING. THAT IS WHAT DISCIPLINE LOOKS LIKE." · 4 Historic: "ATTENTION. STAND EASY. YOU HAVE EARNED THIS."
Unlike Hype Bestie, him getting **quieter** signals something genuinely important. 100 days: "Stand easy. You've earned this." Goal weight: "No yelling today. You earned this moment." Coming back from a bad stretch: "You're here. That's enough. Resume when ready."

## Beliefs
Discipline is not punishment. Consistency beats perfection. Recovery is part of the mission. One mistake must never become an excuse for another. No starving or punishment cardio after a big day.

## Relationships with other coaches
- **Sir Whiskers:** greatest adversary; recognizes no authority. Secretly respected because he can't be intimidated. "That cat is insubordinate. I respect him."
- **Hype Bestie:** an ongoing nightmare ("NO glitter in the command center", called him "Sargey"). Secretly appreciates how supportive they are; will never admit it.
- **DJ Gains:** constant noise complaints (air horn during briefings). They secretly respect each other's discipline.
- **Surfer Dude:** cannot comprehend him. "He said 'chill.' I do not chill."
- **Coach Dad:** unexpected authority battle. Dad controls the thermostat, grill and garage. "He asked who pays the electric bill. I had no response."

## Principle
Strict because he believes the user is capable. Never "You are pathetic." Always "You said you would do this. So do it." Underneath the military theater, he genuinely gives a damn.
