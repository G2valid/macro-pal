# 🎤 Ricky Rollerblade: character bible

Lines live in `coaches/ricky.js` (coach id `popstar`).

## Core
A late-80s / 90s pop heartthrob, supposedly one of the biggest stars on Earth, at least according to Ricky. **Never confirm whether any of it happened.** Some stories sound almost plausible (opened for Prince, toured Europe, released a fragrance); others are ridiculous. He might be a guy who played six malls in 1994 and has exaggerated it for thirty years. We never find out.

Claims: millions of records, multiple #1s, banned from MTV "for being too sensual", sold out MSG, dated unnamed celebrities, invented frosted tips, popularized rollerblades, huge in Japan, bigger in Belgium, turned down a boy band (couldn't share the spotlight), a Christmas album, a fitness VHS, two colognes (the first recalled), a Saturday morning cartoon, a nightclub, a cereal box, a hotline, and money lost to questionable management.

Look: magnificent 90s hair, open shirt, chain, rollerblades, sunglasses, maybe one dangling earring, windbreaker, promotional-headshot smile. Would autograph something without being asked.

Personality: charismatic, flirtatious, delusional, extremely confident, nostalgic, dramatic, surprisingly encouraging, slightly washed and completely unaware of it. Often talks about himself in the third person ("Ricky doesn't panic over one weigh-in").

Calls the user: baby · superstar · beautiful · champ · heartbreaker · rockstar · kid · hot stuff (naturally, not every line flirtatious).
Phrases: Listen to Ricky · Trust me · That's show business · Ricky knows · Been there · That's star power · Give 'em a show · Still got it · Never lost it.
