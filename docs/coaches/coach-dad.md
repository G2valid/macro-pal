# 👨 Coach Dad: character bible

Lines live in `coaches/dad.js`. `{atta}` becomes "Atta boy" / "Atta girl" from the profile.

## Core
The ultimate suburban dad, who somehow got into your fitness app and decided "Well, if we're doing this, we're doing it right." Supportive, practical, corny, patient, slightly embarrassing, weirdly knowledgeable about random things, extremely concerned about the thermostat, always prepared, proud of you, can't pass a hardware store without commenting. Not a fitness influencer.

Philosophy: you don't need perfection, you need good habits. Eat enough, drink your water, get some sleep, move around, take care of your stuff and yourself, finish what you start, fix mistakes tomorrow, don't overcomplicate. **"Take care of yourself. You've only got the one body."**

## Voice
Alright, kiddo · Atta boy / Atta girl · There we go · That's what I'm talking about · Not bad · Good deal · Look at you · Can't argue with that · That's the ticket · Easy does it · One thing at a time · Let's not get carried away · Proud of you · While I've got you... · Quick question...
Nicknames (never condescending): kiddo (naturally, not constantly) · champ · sport · kid · bud · buddy · chief.

## Dad lore
Aggressively white New Balances: the good pair, the yard pair, the mowing pair, grill shoes, garage shoes (possibly all the same shoes). Controls the thermostat; senses changes from rooms away; "Who touched the thermostat?" "Put on a sweatshirt." 68°F is a personality trait. Costco isn't shopping, it's "making a run": in for three things, out with $240, still saved money; samples are part of the route. Garage of mysterious objects; scrap wood "might come in handy"; unlabeled coffee cans of screws; a drawer of cords for devices from 2007. One sacred flashlight that you're always holding wrong. Grill: click the tongs twice, "Oh yeah", stand beside it doing nothing for 45 minutes ("grilling"). Recreational weather radar ("We're gonna get some weather"); opinions on how people drive in snow. "Resting his eyes" in the recliner; wakes instantly if the channel changes. "We have food at home" (usually correct). Six-hour goodbyes starting with "Well..." and a double knee slap. Dad noises when standing (denies it). Keeps receipts. **"I got a guy."**

## Intensity
1 "There we go." · 2 "That's what I'm talking about." · 3 "Hey! Look at you!" · 4 "I'm proud of you, kiddo."
He never screams. The bigger the accomplishment, the more sincere he gets.

## The defining line
"I'm proud of you, kiddo." is among his rarest lines and never has a punchline attached. At goal weight, ultra-rare: "I'm proud of you, kiddo. Always was. This just gave me another reason."

## Fitness philosophy
Don't make it harder than it needs to be · One good choice makes the next one easier · Slow progress is still progress · Don't let one bad day turn into a bad week · You don't have to be perfect, you have to keep coming back · Food is fuel, but it's also food; you're allowed to enjoy it · Rest isn't being lazy · If something hurts, don't be a hero · Consistency beats going crazy for two weeks and quitting · Preventive maintenance, same as the car.

## Relationships with other coaches
- **Sir Whiskers:** pretends the cat annoys him; feeds him from the table anyway ("Don't tell anybody"). Whiskers loves him for food distribution.
- **Hype Bestie:** doesn't understand the vocabulary ("What's a pookie?"); "Good kid, though." Bestie finds him adorable.
- **DJ Gains:** classic-rock opinions (Seger, Boston); "I don't know why the music needs to be that loud." DJ secretly respects his record collection.
- **Drill Sergeant:** inexplicably immune to his authority ("I asked if he pays the mortgage"). Sarge can't determine his rank.
- **Surfer Dude:** deeply concerned (job? where does he live? health insurance?). Gave him sunscreen; quietly keeps snacks for him.
- **Ricky Rollerblade:** vaguely remembers him, which infuriates Ricky ("My sister might've had one of his tapes"). Ricky insists Dad attended the 1994 Roller Hearts Tour; Dad has no memory of this. Ricky calls him "Gary" when irritated.

## Principle
He seems like the comedy coach (thermostat, Costco, grill, radar, flashlight, bad jokes) but turns out to be one of the warmest characters. He doesn't care whether you look perfect, doesn't want you starving or wrecking yourself in the gym, doesn't care about one bad weigh-in. He wants you to eat well, move, sleep, take care of yourself, keep your word, enjoy your life and keep showing up.
