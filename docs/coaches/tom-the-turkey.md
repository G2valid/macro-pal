# 🦃 Tom the Turkey: holiday coach bible

Lines live in `coaches/tom.js`. Primary appearance: November (holiday guest). Selectable year-round.

## Core
A turkey who has become painfully aware of Thanksgiving. Nervous, paranoid, fast-talking, lovable; suspicious of humans, ovens, and EXTREMELY suspicious of the phrase "lean protein". Weirdly knowledgeable about Thanksgiving cooking. A surprisingly good fitness coach determined to survive November. He knows. Nobody will tell him why every grocery store has pictures of turkeys everywhere.

Voice: "Okay, okay, okay." · Listen · Buddy · Pal · Quick question · Just curious · Hypothetically... · Not that I'm worried · I'm not panicking · WHY WOULD I BE PANICKING? · Keep it moving · Nothing to see here. Accidentally gobbles when stressed ("Gobble— sorry. Reflex."). Calls the user buddy, pal, friend, boss, chief, human, trusted human, and "Protector" during Thanksgiving week.

## Lore
Hates ovens, roasting pans, meat thermometers, basters, carving knives, poultry seasoning and the word "brine". Nervous around rosemary and sage. Doesn't trust butter, November grandmothers, or anyone who says "family recipe". Gravy is evidence of a larger conspiracy; cranberry sauce is guilty by association. Hides at the word "preheating". Claims he's "mostly feathers" with almost no protein (false) and always recommends chicken instead (Whiskers agrees enthusiastically; Tom regrets involving Whiskers). Escape routes, a packed suitcase, an alias ("Tim", fools nobody), a fake mustache, considered posing as a peacock or "vegan". December 1 is basically New Year's Day.

**The protein problem:** "Protein? Great. Chicken." "Poultry? Let's not generalize." "Turkey? Never heard of it." Logging turkey occasionally gets "...what did you just log?" / "I TRUSTED YOU." Pure comedy, never food judgment. (In the app: about half the time a food with "turkey" in its name is logged, at most once a day.)

## The November story (automatic, based on the date)
Thanksgiving = 4th Thursday of November. His phase lines take over greetings and small talk:
- **Nov 1–10 (early):** mostly normal ("Nothing ominous whatsoever"), ~25% of the time.
- **Nov 11–20 (mid):** noticing things (butter, sage), ~40%.
- **Thanksgiving week (6 to 2 days before):** paranoid (roasting pans, cranberries, kitchen twine), ~65%.
- **Thanksgiving Eve:** full panic ("THEY'RE BRINING", "HOW FAST CAN YOU RUN?"), always.
- **Thanksgiving Day:** hiding, also when food is logged ("Do not describe dinner"), always. Going over calories gets the holiday food philosophy instead.
- **Day after:** emerging ("...is it safe?", Black Friday). Then back to normal lines.
- **December 1:** farewell: "December. Beautiful, beautiful December. Santa's problem now."
He drops in as a guest more often as it gets closer: ~35% normally, 60% Thanksgiving week, ~85–90% Eve through the day after.

## Holiday food philosophy (important)
Thanksgiving never produces "you ruined your diet". It's one meal. Enjoy the people and the food. You don't need to earn dinner, starve all day before, or do punishment cardio after. Return to your normal routine.
Defining line: **"It's one meal, buddy. Enjoy your life."** … "Just don't tell me what's on the plate."

## Intensity

## Relationships with other coaches
- **Sir Whiskers:** Tom is terrified; Whiskers sees a food-shaped coworker ("He asked whether I was 'free-range.'"). Whiskers: "The bird appears nervous. I require chicken. …Unless…" Tom: "CHICKEN. YOU REQUIRE CHICKEN."
- **Hype Bestie:** thinks Tom is adorable; Tom fears they'll expose his location ("STOP TAGGING ME", "Thanksgiving Queen").
- **DJ Gains:** deliberately plays ominous songs ("Stayin' Alive", "Run Like Hell", "Hungry Like the Wolf"; "Another One Bites the Dust" crossed a line).
- **Drill Sergeant:** Tom trusts him in November; perimeter and evacuation route, "Operation Gobble" (refuses to rename it). Sarge takes Tom's security weirdly seriously.
- **Surfer Dude:** offered sanctuary in his van ("wherever, bro").
- **Ricky Rollerblade:** promises celebrity protection; the disguise is sunglasses ("RICKY, I'M A TURKEY").
- **Coach Dad:** Tom trusts him most. Dad also owns a carving knife ("layers"), gave him leftovers (didn't ask what kind), and refuses to answer questions about Thanksgiving dinner.
