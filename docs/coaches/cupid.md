# 💘 Cupid: holiday coach bible

Lines live in `coaches/cupid.js`. Holiday guest Feb 1–15; selectable year-round.

## Core
An ancient professional matchmaker completely exhausted by humanity: red flags, texting exes, "you up?" at 1:37 AM, confusing attention with affection, swearing off dating then downloading the apps again. Flirty, sarcastic, charming, ancient, slightly cynical, gossipy, dramatic, surprisingly wise, extremely pro-self-respect. He still believes in love; he's just disappointed in how humans handle it.

Philosophy: "You can't control who notices you. You can control how you treat yourself." Fitness should not be an attempt to become attractive enough to deserve love. Take care of yourself because you matter. Confidence, self-respect, keeping promises to yourself and boundaries are attractive. (Drinking water too. He didn't make the rules.)

Voice: Darling · Sweetheart · Beautiful · Heartbreaker · Love · Listen to Cupid · Oh, honey · Absolutely not · Darling, please · We need to talk · I've been doing this for thousands of years. Playful flirting; **never sexualizes the user's body.**

## Lore
Runs "Romantic Operations" (bow, arrows, emergency arrows, backup bow, relationship files, millennia of grievances). The arrows are "extremely precise"; they are not (Athens, Rome, Paris, the wrong twin, at least one war he won't name; yes, he has a supervisor). Files on everyone's dating history; claims confidentiality, gossips anyway. Hates dating apps; uses them for "market research"; profile age 2,847; reported as a bot. "Situationship" is humanity's worst invention; "wyd" is unacceptable; ghosting is cowardly (unless they're dangerous); double texting isn't fatal. Vague historical romance gossip only (no fake claims about real historical relationships).

## Valentine's countdown (automatic)
Feb 1–7 relaxed (~30%) · Feb 8–12 busy (~45%) · Feb 13 "STOP PANIC-BUYING FLOWERS" · Valentine's morning "SHOWTIME" · Valentine's evening chaos ("Someone proposed at Applebee's") · Feb 15 half-price chocolate. Guest frequency rises to ~85% on the day.
On Valentine's Day he adds lines for **partnered** or **single** people only if the person chose that in coach settings (default "Don't ask"). Being single is never framed as failure: "Cupid is a matchmaker, not a hostage negotiator."

## Valentine's food philosophy
Eat the chocolate; chocolate isn't betrayal; dinner isn't a moral test; no skipping meals to "save calories" for love; no punishment cardio; enjoy dessert, return to normal. Healthy relationships include flexibility, including with food.
